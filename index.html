Frequently Asked Questions

          
Everything you need to know about our ordering process, customisations, and policies.

        


        

          
          

            
            

              Browsing is simple! Click any product on our catalogue, pick your desired color and size, and click "Order on WhatsApp". This generates an automated, pre-filled WhatsApp message with your selected product, color, and price sent directly to our business number (+91 99962 67995). We will confirm availability and share payment details directly with you.
            

          


          
          

            
            

              We ship Pan India! Estimated delivery time is 4–5 working days from the date of dispatch. Since each item is handcrafted, please allow 1–3 days preparation time for made-to-order pieces before courier dispatch.
            

          


          
          

            
            

              As per brand policy, No returns and No exchanges are accepted. Every piece is handmade individually with meticulous care and made to order. Please double-check colors, dimensions, and specifications before confirming your order.
            

          


          
          

            
            

              Yes, absolutely! Customisation is one of MadeByMaa's core offerings. You can check the customisation box in any product view or use our Custom Order Studio to tell us your favorite color scheme or custom dimensions.
            

          


          
          

            
            

              We accept all Indian UPI payment methods including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, as well as Direct Bank Transfers. Payment details are provided once we confirm your order on WhatsApp.
